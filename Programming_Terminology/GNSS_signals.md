GNSS signal components:
- Carrier - Radio frequency sinusoidal signal at a given frequency.
- Ranging (PRN) code - Signal of 1's of 0's. Allows receiver to determine the travel time from the satellite. This is a [[Pseudo-Random_Noise]] sequence. 2 kinds of PRN codes are modulated on GPS signals: [[Coarse_Acquisition]] and [[Precision_code]].
- Navigation data - Binary coded message providing information on the [[ephemeris]], clock-bias parameters, [[Almanac_information]], satellite health information.
