This teaches children to teach themselves, valuing their skills, and those of others.

Students work in groups. Groups have [[cross-functionality]]. This is enabled by giving them personality quiz on day 1 (questions on: math enjoyment, goal centred, caring...) and choosing teams with varied answers. This helps students value strengths in others (key skill).

Make a scrum board. Give post-it notes out at start of each lesson. Tell students the exam dates. Students can choose which topics to do, and they teach each other. Students estimate task sizes using [[Planning_poker]]. Teacher just assists. Students even organize homework!

Class does a [[retrospective]] at the end.
