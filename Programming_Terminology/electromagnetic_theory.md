**Electric charge**
It is conserved. It can be transferred between objects through contact or induction.
Unit - Coulomb

**Electric field**
The ratio of a the force on a test charge to the magnitude of the test charge.
Unit - N/C or V/m
Coulombs law: ![[electric_field_equation_img.png]] k = 8.99*(10^9)
Can also express in terms of electric potential: The work done per unit charge to to move test charge from infinity to a point in space. ![[electric_potential_equation_img.png]]

**Magnetic field**
Direction is given by the right hand rule. (Point thumb towards north pole).
Unit - Tesla
The magnetic field of a long, straight current-carrying wire is given by Ampere's law: ![[amperes_law_eqn_img.png]] mu0 = 4 * pi * (10^-7). r = perpendicular distance from wire. Another form of Ampere's law is **Maxwell equation 4** ![[maxwell_eqn_4_img.png]] (for EM waves) where i is current, c is the speed of light and E is electric field
Magnetic field due to a permanent magnetic is shown in the magnetic diploe model: ![[mag_dip_mom_eqn_img.png]] m is the magnetic dipole moment (points from S to N pole). B is perp to both m and r vectors. Cross product: ![[cross_product_matrix_img.png]]
Magnetic field can be expressed in terms of magnetic vector potential A: ![[mag_vec_pot_eqn_img.png]] 

**Electric flux**
Measure of how much electric field passes through an area A: ![[elec_flux_eqn_img.png]] The integral form of this over area ![[gauss_law_integral_form_eqn.png]] is Gauss law which comes out with this: ![[gauss_law_eqn_img.png]] **Maxwell equation 1** where eps = 8.85 x 10^-12
Can use this to calculate electric field due to symmetric charge distributions (spheres, cylinders, planes)

**Magnetic flux**
![[mag_flux_eqn_img.png]] if we integrate this over area (like with Gauss law) then we come out with **Maxwell equation 2** ![[gauss_law_for_mag_eqn_img.png]]
This implies there are no magnetic monopoles. Magnetic fields are always produced by dipoles or loops of current.

**Electromagnetic induction**
The phenomenon of generating and electric current in a conductor due to a changing magnetic flux. Faradays law **Maxwell equation 3**:![[em_induct_eqn_img.png]]  or in a more usable form: ![[em_induct_eqn_img_2.png]] *E* is the emf (or voltage). N is the number of turns of wire.
This is how generators work (mechanical energy (the coli spinning) changes the magnetic flux the wire experiences, this causes a current to flow (electrical energy)):
![[generator_img.png]] and how transformers work: ![[transformer_em_induc_img.png]]

**Electromagnetic waves**
Oscillating electric and magnetic fields emerge from accelerating charges. ![[velocity_of_EM_wave_eqn.png]] and the energy: ![[energy_of_a_photon_eqn.png]] h = 6.63 x 10^-34
