Africa has been fastest growing region of world in terms of GDP, strong Chinese links. Young population, high fertility.
They must invest in infrastructure and [[human_capital]]. Must improve governance, education, training. 
Most populous countries are Nigeria, Ethiopia, DRofC, South Africa. Their progress is mirrored by their smaller neighbours.
Communications between African countries is poor (inc. roads). China is improving it from it's [[Belt_and_road_initiative]]. The continent suffers from an
[[Economic_growth_paradox]].

Sub-saharan -
World's poorest region

Nigeria - 
Population 207 million. Overly reliant on oil production. At the bottom end of middle-income countries.
They must find other revenue sources. Challenging, as 2/3 population are agriculture trained only. Emigration will increase. High entrepreneurial drive.

Ghana - 
Population 27 million. 2nd fastest growing African economy. 
Very dependent on gold and petrol.
Mainly agriculture workforce, have begun diversifying into manufacturing and services.

Ethiopia -
Population 100 million. One of fastest growing world economies. Began from very poor base. Must push on to reach middle-income status. 
Have had much aud from abroad. Must wrangle social and ethnic tensions. 
Ok tourism, must improve manufacturing to become [[industrialised]].

DRofC -
Main exports are minerals (copper, cobalt). Very poor due to government mismanagement and political instability, Rwanda war. 

Gabon, equatorial Guinea-
Oil rich, yet mass inequality (half population have clean drinking water).

South Africa -
Pros - Africa's richest economy. Best educated workforce, many natural resources, strong service industries (tourism, finance), strong manufacturing.
Cons - slow economic growth. Unemployment is 27%, and inequality gives crime and murder. Stained legacy of [[apartheid]]. Low business confidence. [[Economic_growth_paradox]].
