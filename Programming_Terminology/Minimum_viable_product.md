(MVP) example...

Scenario: Somebody is tired of walking to work. One option is to give them a regular car, the other is to do MVP. 

MVP: You first present a skateboard (minimum upgrade possible), but they fall off often.
So you present a scooter, but it's too slow.
Then a bicycle, but it's still too slow.
Then a motorbike. They loved wind in their face, but want to bring a friend sometimes.
So you present a convertible car. They love it.

Conclusion: The [[Convertible]] is better than the regular car option. We found this out because MVP showed us that the customer liked the [[wind]]!
