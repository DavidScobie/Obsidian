User story - Focus on 1) who is doing task, 2) what they are doing, and 3) the desired outcome/motivation. 
E.g. "As a special forces medic, I must teach basic physiology to my students, so they can understand the human body"
Stories should adhere to INVEST-
I : Independent of other stories
N : Negotiable (able to be rewritten)
V : it should add Value to the business
E : The story size should be Estimable
S : Small (If too big, break it up)
T : Make a Test to check completion
