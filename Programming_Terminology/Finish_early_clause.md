Imagine a project costing customer 1mil/month for 20 months (paid monthly).

This clause is where: If the project gets finished before 20 months, the customer stops paying the monthly fee, but pays 20% of the remainder (if it took 20 months).

If project finishes early (3 months), the customer pays less (than forecasted) and gets the product quicker. Also the scrum team receives more money per time than they originally planned!

This way both customer and scrum team try to work efficiently, as they both win out this way.
