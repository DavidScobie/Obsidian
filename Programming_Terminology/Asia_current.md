
China - 
In the last 50 years it has gone from one of world's poorest, to a very average country. They went from a [[command_economy]] to a [[market_economy]]. They've copied western goods (cars) and services (social media and search engine).
Pros - world pioneers in solar energy, and electric cars

Hong Kong - very rich

Singapore - 3rd highest GDP per head. 
Pros - ease of doing business, economic freedom, education.

Taiwan - average level economy, export to china and Hong Kong 
Pros - electronics manufacturing.
Cons - failed to create global brands

India -
Economy is growing fast. It's richest state is equivalent to Bulgaria, it's poorest state ranks as low as Mali. Current GDP per head is 1/4 of china. Growing population, infrastructure investment.
Pros - very well-educated elite. Many skilled young people. Good service and manufacturing industries. 

Bangladesh, Pakistan, Sri Lanka -
High populations. Tensions **suck resources into [[Defense]] spending**.
Sri Lanka is a middle-income country.
Pakistan (export rice) and Bangladesh (export clothes) are 2 of the fastest growing economies in the world.
Cons - government, environmental issues, water shortage. They'll need good education, health and social services for a [[sustainable]] growing populus. 

Japan -
Oldest population on earth. Stagnating economy (was great until the 90's). Toyota good still, but Sony, Panasonic, Toshiba dropped off. 
Pros - Low crime, high quality of services, cleanliness.

North Korea - [[command_economy]] which struggles to feed it's people.

South Korea - very energetic [[market_economy]]. Good car and smartphone exporters. 
Cons - weak in service industry. Very few young people.
