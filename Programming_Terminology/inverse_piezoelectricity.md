Apply an electric voltage to a [[piezoelecrtic_material]]. A mechanical pressure occurs which leads to a change in the material's length.

When the voltage is turned off, the material returns to original size.
The voltage on-off rate can be precisely controlled. Therefore the material vibrates very precisely, and acts as a control ([[reference_oscillator]]).

It acts like an [[RLC_circuit]].

The L's are [[inductor]]s and the C is a [[capacitor]] (which adjusts the frequency)
L1, L2 and L3 make a [[transformer]]. Induced emf from L1 and L2 is inducted into L3.
We get an alternating [[emf]] going into the crystal.
Therefore effectively the transistor, capacitor, inductor setup (like an [[RLC_circuit]]) makes [[resonance]] emf, going into the crystal, making it vibrate at a well-defined frequency.
