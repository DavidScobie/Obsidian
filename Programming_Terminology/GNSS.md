Global Navigation Satellite System.

There are 4/5 main types of GNSS (for different countries): [[GPS]], [[GLONASS]], [[Galileo]], [[BeiDou-3]] (and [[QZSS]]). Each has > 24 Medium-Earth-Orbit satellites.

Signals broadcast from a GNSS satellite allow the receiver to know its: **position**; **navigate** from one point to another; **timestamp** events to a fine precision (10 nanoseconds).

GNSS systems provide: free signals for civilian use (in phones, fitness watches, cars, trains...); high-precision, encrypted signals that governments use to protect critical functions.

Energy substations use GNSS signals for the precise timing data to synchronize operations across energy grids. And Cell towers use this to synchronize wireless communication networks.
