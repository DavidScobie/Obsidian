#book
London - Police surveillance with facial recognition cameras to help find criminals. But Londoners concerned at feeling of being watched. Protests.

Scotland - A girl had pictures of her used to make a [[deepfake]] (AI porn where her face put on someone else's body), very distressing. No law against it at the time, she worked to get the law changed.

Nigeria - A [[content_moderator]] had a horrible task of trawling through horrible images (paedophilic, murder...) to train AI algorithm to not share them on Facebook. Little pay, long working hours, expendable workforce, no-one to complain to.

Netherlands - Lady whose teenage son committed a crime and got put on a criminal register from [[predictive_policing]]. Family then got bombarded with social workers, and were constantly monitored. This puts stress on the family, which makes it more likely that he'll re-offend. It is also very hard to get off the register.

America - Taxi driver for Uber in San Francisco, part of the [[gig_economy]] There was a fault in the app where driver's weren't being paid enough as their distance travelled on the map (in the app) was an underestimation. The man set up his own app (Uber cheats) which other users joined, to fight Uber and reclaim pay. Again, no direct person to go to (as complaints managed by AI chat-bot).

India - Female doctor working in very rural, poor region. Hospitals take a day to travel to, and TB is prevalent. AI [[medical_image_algorithm]] can determine if hospitalisation necessary. But the patient can't afford the journey anyway. Issue is that income from AI goes to a few huge companies, not poor communities. This is [[data_colonialism]]. 

Argentina - Poor region in the north where rape is rife. Government paid for Microsoft to train ML algorithm using social factors to determine which individuals should be given government support. Algorithm was never finished. Even if it got finished, it's accuracy would have been questionable.

China - [[Government_surveillance]] with facial recognition cameras to build up a reputation score for you. If you perform badly, you cannot leave your town. A lady was jailed for 2 years for installing WhatsApp (only WeChat is allowed). Dystopian society where everyone is watched.

 [[Artistic_job_displacement]]- Artists and voice-actors losing work because AI can take their place. Law needs changing whereby people can opt out of their material being used to train algorithms. 

Vatican (epilogue) - Advisor to the pope organised the religious leaders of catholic, Jewish and Islam churches to sign an agreement for [[ethical_AI]] (that AI must be used to aid welfare). This was discussed with the leaders of the biggest AI companies (Microsoft, meta, FB/Insta, deep-mind...).
