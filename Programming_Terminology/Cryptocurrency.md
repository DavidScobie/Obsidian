A pro is that it isn't monitored by govt, so it avoids tax, hence it's recent boom. 
But a currency must satisfy 3 things: 'medium of exchange', 'unit of account', 'store of value', and crypto doesn't meet any of these. Also crypto is not authorised by any country. Hence crypto won't replace govt currencies (euro, pound...).
Crypto should be instead seen as an asset class (like gold).
