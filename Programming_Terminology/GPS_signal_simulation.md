**Normal GNSS operation**
GPS has 24 operational satellites and 8 for maintenance and redundancy. These are in 6 orbital planes, such that 4-6 are always visible. Data is needed from 4 for and accurate 'fix'.
Precise timestamps (from atomic clocks) along with positional data are sent from satellite to receiver. Closer satellite signal data arrives faster. Use trilateration to pinpoint.

**GPS signal simulator**
The simulator produces >3 discreet signals.
'scenarios' are presented to receiver to test: signal acquisition, data retention, overall performance.
Simulators make signals from scratch, or play recordings from real satellites.
