**Jake MPhys lit review and project writeup** – Conflict with a colleague, and resolution
Situation: Myself and my partner were tasked with writing up a report on the project we had just completed.
Task: We received drastically different marks in our lit review earlier on, hence we didn’t know if we should work together or independently as we thought our writing styles may differ.
Action: I suggested that we read over each other’s lit reviews so that we could ascertain why we got the different marks.
Result: It turned out that our writing styles were the same. He had just ordered his material wrongly, hence the differing marks. Therefore we wrote our report together and got a good result.

**EMG students had already learned the lesson** – Take initiative to improve situation
Situation: I needed to take a cover double lesson in a new school, with a class I had never taught before.
Task: I checked the lesson scheduler beforehand, and chose to teach them ‘Space’ as it hadn’t been recorded as taught to them yet. However, 2 minutes into the lesson I realised they had been taught ‘Space’ yesterday!
Action: I asked my AC (Assistant Coordinator) to take charge for a few minutes while I went next door and realised they had the same issue. I then phoned the office to ask them to bring down the ‘Hydraulic-Judo-Bots’ practical materials for us both. I then taught Pythagoras (requiring no material) for the first lesson, and gave them the Hydraulic-Judo-Bots practical for the 2nd lesson.
Result: We only wasted 5-10 minutes out of the 90 minute double period, and the children didn’t mind.
