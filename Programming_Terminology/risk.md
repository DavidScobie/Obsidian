3 types of business risk:

Market risk: Do customers want to buy it?
This is sorted in the [[Minimum_viable_product]] cycles.

Technical risk: Can we build it?
This is sorted by building prototypes of possible products before mass production. These can be tested, and tweaked as necessary.

Financial risk: Will we make profit overall?
[[WeWork_story]]... Be sure to deliver what customer wants in incremental releases, instead of investing in [[5-Waste]].
