Only act on ideas meeting these 3 criteria:
What you can [[implement]]
What you can [[sell]]
What you are [[passionate]] about
At the start of enterprise, make a [[backlog]]. 80% of this backlog is [[5-Waste]] so you prioritise items which are easiest and generate the most revenue. 
The [[product_manager]] is in charge of the backlog.
[[OODA]] - Observe Orient Decide Act
[[Minimum_viable_product]] uses OODA cycles to tailor the product to the customer's wants/needs.
Don't prioritise too many projects, as this renders each priority average.
When to [[release]] the product?
[[Change_for_free]] instead of [[Change_fees]].
This all helps avoid [[risk]].
The first steps to using scrum yourself are having a team and making a backlog.
