Magnetisation starts as longitudinal (+z). Then apply a 90 degree pulse (mag goes to to x-y plane).
Spins then spread at different speeds and relax back up to +z direction.
Then apply 180 degree pulse. This sends the spins to -z direction.
Spins relax back up, and slow ones are caught up by fast ones, causing an echo (transverse net magnetisation)
