Origins: Paper written by Stern stating its economically better to consider climate change sooner than later. This lead to 2015 Paris agreement. The Greta Thunberg aroused the masses. Companies began to consider [[ESG_investing]] more carefully. Elon Musk helped the whole movement with Tesla.

Food and water - 
If there's enough water, food can be grown.
Draught-resistant crops will help.
Distribution of land and food matter more than supply.
Vegetarian food is less wasteful than meat.
Complications around growing palm plantations (as these can make biodiesel).

Energy supply -
Rapid wind and solar development.
Falling cost of batteries.
UK doesn't use much energy as it exports mainly services, not goods
The emerging world will use more energy, it's important that they use renewables.
[[Nuclear_power]] has been a failure, due to: high costs, safety risks and local objections.
