They can be: reflected, refracted or absorbed.
Temperature and atmospheric pressure - causes the atmosphere to expand, changing refractive index, causing bending
Rain and humidity - Water droplets absorb, scatter and reflect the waves
Snow - Absorbs and scatters the waves
Lightning - The strong charge makes an electric field which repels the RF wave
