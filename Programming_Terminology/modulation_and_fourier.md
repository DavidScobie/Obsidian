Radio waves (BBC radio 2) are sent as a low frequency (audible wave) along with a high frequency modulation.
When the receiver picks up this signal, it is fourier transformed into the low and high components. 
Then a low-pass filter gets rid of the high frequency, and the listener is left with only the audible, low frequency wave.

Perhaps a similar approach could be used with GNSS signals? Where we use a high-pass filter to keep only the RF EM waves.
