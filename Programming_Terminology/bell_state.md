If the spins are up (U) and down (D) for instance then the state is (UD + DU). For each, the probability of measuring U or D would be 50%.
We take one electron to Pluto.
If we measure the electron on earth and it is U, then the one on Pluto **has** to be D. This shows that the system is instantaneously changing.

There are a total of 4 bell states (UU, DD, UD and DU).
