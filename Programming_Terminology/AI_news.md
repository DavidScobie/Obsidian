**26/5/25 - 
BBC News - AI could already be conscious. Are we ready for it? - BBC News**
https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c0k3700zljjo
Sussex uni have made [[dreamachine]] which examines brain activity (by measuring electrical signals and blood flow to certain cortical regions) and used [[LLM]]'s to explore how thought processes work. 
Worryingly, the way in which LLM's work is poorly understood.
Pittsburgh uni have connected LLM's with sensory inputs (vision and touch) and cameras to construct an [[internal_language]] which could rival real [[consciousness]]. 
Cortical labs (Australian firm) have made [[Cerebral_organoids]] which play the game 'pong'. 
As AI develops, we will develop [[empathy]] for it, which will distort our [[moral_priorities]] away from other humans.
