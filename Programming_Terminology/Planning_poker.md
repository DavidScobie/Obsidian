Many rooms of a house needs to be painted.
Everyone involved chooses a [[Fibonacci_weighting]] for the length of time to paint each room.
If all choices are within 2 values, then average them.
If choices span 3 or more values, the people who said the highest and lowest justify their decision. Then everyone chooses again, (until max span of 2), then average.
It's important that those doing the job do the choosing, as only they know their skills.
