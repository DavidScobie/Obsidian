GNSS spoofing - interference tactic that uses deceptive signals to mislead aircraft navigation systems, causing false positioning data.

Spirient have made an eBook on this which addresses short-term threats:
- **Short-term**: Real-time spoof detection and alerting system (firmware updates)
- **Medium-term**: Upgrading navigation hardware to support multiple satellite constellations and frequencies; use non-GNSS sensors to as a reference for the GNSS sensors.
- **Long-term**: Exploring the potential for low-earth orbit (LEO) PNT services as an alternative and/or complementary source of information.

This eBook offers a balanced view, weighing the pros and cons of each approach.
The regulations and requirements governing air travel mean that many of these strategies will take time to assess, qualify, and certify.
