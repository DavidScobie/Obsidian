**Travel information**
Quite difficult to get to: multiple flights (Bali to Jakarta then Jakarta to Padang); long boat journey (Padang to Mentawai Island Regency 3-6 hours)
Mentawai surf tax: $150USD for up to 15 days (mandatory to pay this!)
More expensive than other Indonesia locations

**Surf guide**
One of the world's largest wave playgrounds, with hundreds of surf breaks. 
Arguably the small, tropical islands have the most consistent surf on the planet
Left and rights, hollow tubes and steep walls - Good for intermediate and advanced surfers
Best time to surf is March-November

**Trip types**
Surf charter boat trip - Boat takes you to different breaks (best for large groups)
Surf camp - Best for solo-travellers, small groups
