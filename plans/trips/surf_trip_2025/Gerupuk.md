Home to 3 reef breaks:
Don Don - Beginner friendly
Gerupuk Inside - Good for intermediates, breaking over a soft, sand bottom
Gerupuk Outside - Good for intermediate and advanced. Fast A-frame that breaks over a shallow reef
Accessible by boat only
Great for working on cut-backs and deep bottom turns against a spectacular backdrop
