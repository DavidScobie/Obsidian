**Travel information**
From Kuta generally 30-40 minute drive, and then maybe 20 minute boat out to the breaks (quite long)
Boats to the breaks cost $15
From Bali you can either get $30 ferry, or $80 flight. Considering other expenses (like taxi etc...) it may be better to fly
Not safe to travel outside Kuta alone at night time

**Surf guide**
As Bali is overcrowded, many are heading here now, but can still be crowded
South coast has most of the surf spots
There is something for all surf levels here
Mostly mellow, easy to surf breaks
