#psychacks 
[[Emotional_maturity]] happens around age 30.
At this age most people start to wake up from their unwanted thoughts. Much like [[decluttering]] a house. It's hard to let go of these thoughts due to emotional attachment (dad's idea, friend's opinion..). 
It takes time to clean the house, but if you don't, you're a [[pastiche]], feeling unfulfilled, and not knowing who you are.
To treat it, [[turn_inward]] and question your own conscious thoughts. You want your thoughts to be consciously chosen by you. It gets easier with time, and along this process you'll want to restrict the flow of new information in, like a [[valve]]. 
The news and social media can be expelled as it's arguably junk for the mind (as it is often untrue or not useful).
