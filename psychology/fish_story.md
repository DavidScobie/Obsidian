Men fish for sport (not food), some fish are easier to catch than others. Once caught, fish are thrown back into river.

Most fish are ok being thrown back, but some will be upset. These ones will try and jump back into the boat.

Men should only have relationships with fish who jump back into the boat, as they've made an effort ([[pot_comitted]]).

A fish who doesn't jump back into the boat, wants you to join it at the bottom of the cold, dark water. The fish know this, that's why they let themselves get caught. These fish are not worth getting wet for. 

If no fish are being caught, keep on fishing, it'll work eventually.
