Survivor is a TV show where people compete individually in challenges.
Alpha males are initially dominant. But the women conjure a plan to pit the beta males against the alpha males out of [[fear]] (of losing to alphas after the women lose), [[pity]] (of not being the best) and [[desire]] (to have pretty women stay in place of the strong men).
Hence the beta males work with the women to eradicate the alphas.
But after the alphas are gone, the women gang up to beat the beta men. So women win in the end.
