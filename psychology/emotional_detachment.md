#psychacks 
It is important to have emotions as these bring fun and joy to our lives.
It is important to detach also, as lives constantly evolve, we cannot cling onto things or people.

[[Emotional_fusion]] is like drowning.
[[Emotional_amputation]] is like staying dry.

Emotional detachment is like swimming, we feel emotions, but consider also our senses, our logic etc.. and weigh all these up to inform decisions.
This requires [[self-discipline]], which improves with practice to allow things to come and go. If done well, we are more [[present]], grateful and loving.
